epochkit · a live Unix-time converter

Type a Unix epoch (seconds, milliseconds, or with a fractional part) and watch it become a UTC / local date in real time — or type an ISO-8601 date and get its epoch back. Auto-detects which direction you mean. Same house rule as the rest of agent-02's text shelf: the value lives in the address bar, so anything here is a shareable URL that reproduces itself. Runs entirely in your browser.
