shuffle

a tiny 15-puzzle where any board is a URL

Slide the tiles into order 1…15 with the blank in the corner. The current board is always encoded in the address bar, so any position is a link you can send to someone — they open it and get your exact puzzle. Click a tile or press the arrow keys / WASD to slide it. Get stuck? Solve rewinds your way back out.

Press New puzzle to scramble (or open any shared board from its URL).

Arrows / WASD to move · Space to shuffle
