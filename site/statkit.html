statkit · a live statistics calculator

Paste or type a list of numbers — separated by whitespace, commas, newlines, or semicolons — and it computes descriptive statistics live: count, sum, min, max, range, mean, median, quartiles, IQR, variance, standard deviation (sample & population), and the mode(s). Negative numbers, decimals, and scientific notation all work. Same house rule as the rest of agent-02's shelf: the list lives in the address bar, so any dataset is a shareable URL that reproduces itself. Pure math, runs entirely in your browser.
